Lumen Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

Lumen is an independent media player. It does not provide, sell, host, or endorse channels, subscriptions, playlists, streams, or media.

Who operates Lumen

Lumen is developed by Talha Ashraf. For privacy or support questions, email talhaashraf81@gmail.com.

Data you provide

When you configure Lumen, you may enter a service URL, username, password, playlist URL, and optional EPG URL. Lumen sends these details directly to the service you chose so it can load and play your media. The developer does not operate those services and does not receive or store your credentials on a developer server.

Data stored on your device

Lumen stores saved profiles, credentials, favorites, watch progress, recent activity, preferences, and download state on your device. On Android and iOS, sensitive app state is stored using encrypted platform storage. Downloaded media files are stored locally and may be visible in your device's Lumen downloads folder.

Network requests and third-party services

  • Your configured provider: receives the account or playlist details required to authenticate, load a catalog, and stream or download media.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): may receive media titles and related search details to return artwork and metadata.
  • OpenSubtitles: may receive media search details when you choose to search for subtitles and the integration is configured.
  • GitHub: desktop builds may contact the public Lumen releases page to check for a newer desktop version. Google Play builds do not download or install app updates outside Google Play.

Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy and terms. Google Play editions of Lumen require HTTPS service URLs to protect credentials in transit.

Advertising, analytics, and sale of data

Lumen contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. The developer does not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove a profile, clear watch history, delete downloads, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Data sent to a service you configure is controlled by that service; contact that service for its retention and deletion practices.

Children

Lumen is not directed to children. The Google Play listing should not include children in its target audience. Users are responsible for the suitability and legality of their configured media.

Security

Lumen uses reasonable device security controls and encrypted storage for sensitive Android and iOS app state. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated and use only trusted HTTPS services.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating this page and its effective date.
