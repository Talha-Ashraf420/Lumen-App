Lumen is a private media player for services and playlists you already have
the legal right to use. Connect your own authorized HTTPS
Xtream-compatible service or M3U playlist and browse the media made
available by that source.

Lumen does not provide, sell, host, curate, or endorse channels,
subscriptions, playlists, streams, movies, series, or any other media. You
must supply your own authorized source. Lumen is not affiliated with any
content provider.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse the live, movie, and series sections supplied by your source
• Search, filter, sort, save favorites, and continue watching
• View an optional XMLTV program guide when your source supports it
• Use optional metadata enrichment and subtitle search integrations

PLAYBACK
• Select available audio and subtitle tracks
• Adjust subtitle appearance, playback speed, video fit, and sleep timer
• Continue with Picture-in-Picture and the in-app mini player
• Save offline copies only where your source and content rights permit it

PERSONALIZED AND PRIVATE
• Light, dark, and system appearance modes with selectable accent colors
• Multiple locally stored source profiles
• Encrypted local credential storage on Android
• No advertising SDK and no analytics SDK

BUILT FOR YOUR SCREENS
• Responsive phone and tablet layouts
• Android TV and Google TV support with remote and D-pad navigation

Availability of sections, metadata, program information, subtitles, and
playback features depends on the source you configure. Users are responsible
for ensuring that they have permission to access and play every source and
media item they add.
